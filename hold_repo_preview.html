What this is

HOLD: 24 Hours is a single-day somatic container — a structured, body-based framework for nervous system regulation that you move through across one full day.

It is not a meditation app. It is not a mood tracker. It is a regulation toolkit grounded in polyvagal theory, breathwork science, and somatic practice.

The framework

Phase Window Focus
H — Hold the morning06:00–10:00Orienting, grounding, baseline check-in
O — Open the body10:00–14:00Movement, breath, posture reset
L — Land in the afternoon14:00–18:00Regulation tools, spike response
D — Descend into rest18:00–22:00Downregulation, sleep preparation
